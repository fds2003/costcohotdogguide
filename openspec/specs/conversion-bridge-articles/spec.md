## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Supply chain efficiency article exists at /articles/supply-chain-efficiency/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/supply-chain-efficiency/` analyzing how Costco's supply chain enables the $1.50 hot dog price, with a bridge to efficiency tools.

#### Scenario: Article explains supply chain with data
- **WHEN** a user reads the supply chain article
- **THEN** the article includes specific data points about inventory turnover, supplier relationships, and volume economics

#### Scenario: Article includes natural tool recommendation
- **WHEN** a user reaches the end of the supply chain article
- **THEN** a natural CTA suggests spreadsheet tools for tracking personal inventory or expenses

### Requirement: Pricing strategy playbook exists at /articles/pricing-strategy/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/pricing-strategy/` with 7 business lessons from Costco's pricing strategy applicable to small businesses.

#### Scenario: Article presents 7 actionable lessons
- **WHEN** a user reads the pricing strategy article
- **THEN** 7 distinct lessons are presented with real Costco examples and actionable takeaways

#### Scenario: Article includes pricing template CTA
- **WHEN** a user finishes reading the pricing strategy article
- **THEN** a CTA offers a free pricing model spreadsheet template

### Requirement: Membership cost-benefit analysis exists at /articles/membership-worth-it/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/membership-worth-it/` with a detailed cost-benefit analysis of Costco membership tiers.

#### Scenario: Article breaks down membership ROI
- **WHEN** a user reads the membership article
- **THEN** a clear breakdown of Gold Star vs Executive membership costs, rewards, and break-even calculations is shown

#### Scenario: Article links to existing calculator
- **WHEN** a user reads the membership article
- **THEN** a prominent link to the existing `/savings/membership-calculator/` is provided

### Requirement: The $1.50 Miracle article exists at /articles/150-miracle/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/150-miracle/` telling the story of why Costco's hot dog price hasn't changed in 40 years.

#### Scenario: Article tells compelling story
- **WHEN** a user reads the $1.50 Miracle article
- **THEN** a narrative arc from 1985 to present explains the business and cultural significance

#### Scenario: Article is highly shareable
- **WHEN** a user shares the article on social media
- **THEN** Open Graph meta tags provide compelling title, description, and image

### Requirement: Complete food court menu guide exists at /articles/food-court-menu/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/food-court-menu/` with a complete listing of all Costco Food Court items, prices, and calories.

#### Scenario: Article lists all menu items
- **WHEN** a user visits the food court menu article
- **THEN** a comprehensive table shows all items with prices and calorie counts

#### Scenario: Article includes price comparison
- **WHEN** a user views the menu article
- **THEN** a section compares Costco Food Court prices to similar fast-food chains

### Requirement: Employee retention article exists at /articles/employee-retention/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/employee-retention/` exploring why Costco employees stay for decades.

#### Scenario: Article covers employee benefits
- **WHEN** a user reads the employee retention article
- **THEN** specific data on wages, benefits, turnover rates, and culture is presented

### Requirement: Is Costco hot dog healthy analysis exists at /articles/is-it-healthy/
The system SHALL generate `/articles/is-it-healthy/` with an evidence-based analysis of whether the Costco hot dog is healthy.

#### Scenario: Article analyzes with data
- **WHEN** a user reads the health analysis article
- **THEN** multiple perspectives (nutritionist, consumer, comparison to alternatives) are presented with data

### Requirement: All bridge articles include natural Affiliate CTAs
Each bridge article SHALL include at least one natural, context-appropriate Affiliate product recommendation or tool CTA.

#### Scenario: At least one CTA per article
- **WHEN** a user reads any bridge article
- **THEN** at least one relevant product or tool is recommended with an Affiliate link or internal link

### Requirement: Bridge articles are interlinked with existing content
Each bridge article SHALL link to at least 3 existing pages to distribute link equity.

#### Scenario: Articles have internal links
- **WHEN** a search engine crawls a bridge article
- **THEN** at least 3 internal links to existing pages are present
